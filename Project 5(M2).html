FitFuel: Your Daily Fitness Guide

Welcome to FitFuel! Your daily guide to movement, strength, energy, and flexibility. Explore simple routines, practise good form, and make movement a fun part of your day.

⚡The Energy Starter

  • Jumping jacks — 30 seconds
  • Marching in place — 1 minute
  • Arm circles — 30 seconds

💪 The Strength Builder

  • Wall push-ups — 8–10 repetitions
  • Bodyweight squats — 8–10 repetitions
  • Bird-dog exercise — 6 repetitions per side

🏃 The Cardio Boost

  • Brisk walking — 2 minutes
  • Step-touch side steps — 1 minute
  • Gentle alternating knee lifts — 1 minute

🧘 The Flex & Flow

  • Shoulder rolls — 10 repetitions
  • Gentle standing side stretches — 15 seconds per side
  • Calf stretches against a wall — 15 seconds per side

📌VERY IMPORTANT📌

Follow this routine regularly and make fitness a fun part of your daily life! Stay hydrated, maintain proper form, take breaks whenever needed, and give your body enough time to rest. Remember, consistency is the key to a healthy and energetic lifestyle. Keep moving, stay motivated, and never give up! ⚡
