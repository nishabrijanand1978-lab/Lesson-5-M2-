MY ANIMAL FACT FILE

PANDA

Pandas mainly eat bamboo — Bamboo makes up most of their diet, even though they belong to the order of carnivores.

Pandas have a special “thumb” — Their enlarged wrist bone works like an extra thumb, helping them grip bamboo.

Pandas are excellent climbers — Young pandas especially use trees to escape danger and explore their surroundings.

Pandas can swim! 🌊🐼 Despite their chunky appearance, giant pandas are capable swimmers and can cross streams and rivers when necessary.
